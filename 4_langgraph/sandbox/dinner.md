# Dinner Report: Little Italy

## Restaurant Name:
Little Italy

## Address:
No 220, 4th Floor, Y Block, Near Bounce, Above Nakshtra, Chennai, 600090

## Menu:
Little Italy specializes in authentic Italian cuisine, focusing on vegetarian options. Their menu includes:
- Wood-fired pizzas
- Artisanal pastas
- Appetizers
- Quality wines

## Reviews:
- **Overall Rating:** 3.0/5 on Tripadvisor 
- **Customer Feedback:** Mixed reviews have been noted. Some customers appreciated the ambience but mentioned that the food flavors were inconsistent, with some pasta dishes criticized for being excessively saucy and pizzas not meeting expectations. 
- **Noteworthy Comments:** Some diners expressed disappointment with portion sizes in relation to pricing, but others praised the authenticity of the Italian flavors offered.
