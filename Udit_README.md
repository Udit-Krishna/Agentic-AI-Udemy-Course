# Agentic AI course materials

This repo contains the course materials for the Agentic AI Udemy course by Edward Donner.

## Course Overview

This is a comprehensive, intensive 6 week course that equips students to create and deploy Autonomous Agents for commercial impact. Students will master Agentic AI Architectures, Frameworks and Protocols, such as the remarkable MCP. They will build 8 powerful Agent solutions that can make decisions and take actions in ways that will astonish them.

By the end of the 6 weeks, students will be able to deliver Agentic AI solutions to benefit their clients, their company and their career. The course promises to be a wild ride — at times astounding, at times surreal, but always immensely satisfying.
